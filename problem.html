What's Broken

The Gaps We're Solving

🔍

Algorithmic Harm

Social media algorithms are optimized for engagement, not well-being. They feed teens more of what disturbs them, not what heals them. The business model is in direct conflict with teen mental health.

⚠️ Engagement-first design
🛒

Data Commodification

Teen data is the most valuable commodity in the digital economy. Every app, every chatbot, every "free" service harvests their information — their interests, fears, vulnerabilities — and sells it.

🔓 Privacy as an afterthought
🤖

AI Without Empathy

Existing AI tools for teens are transactional — they answer questions, they don't read emotions. They have no memory, no continuity, no genuine understanding of context or feeling.

❌ No emotional continuity
⚠️

Harmful Content Exposure

Teens encounter self-harm content, eating disorder communities, radicalization pipelines, and misinformation with zero safety filters. Platforms act after damage is done — not before.

🚨 Reactive, not preventive
🕳️

The 2am Gap

Crisis doesn't keep business hours. Teens reach out at 2am when they can't sleep, when anxiety peaks, when human support isn't available. The systems meant to help them are offline.

🕐 No 24/7 human support
😶

Judgment & Stigma

Teens don't seek help because they're afraid of being judged — by adults, by peers, by institutions. Asking for help is framed as weakness, not strength. The result: silent suffering.

🛑 Stigma barrier
