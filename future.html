Roadmap

Where Rei Goes Next

Rei is just the beginning. Here's the roadmap for her growth — and the future we're building toward.

Development Roadmap

The Path Ahead

Coming Soon — 2026

Voice Interaction

Rei will gain a voice — a warm, steady, calming voice. Teens can talk to her hands-free, and she'll respond with synthesized speech that matches her maternal, reassuring tone. Perfect for睡前 conversations or when your hands are full.

Speech Synthesis Voice Recognition Tone Matching
Coming Soon — 2026

Multi-Platform Expansion

Beyond the website — Rei will be available as a Discord bot, a mobile app, and a browser extension. She'll meet teens where they already are, whether that's Discord servers, TikTok browsers, or iOS home screens.

iOS / Android Discord Bot Browser Extension
Mid-Term — 2027

Guardian Dashboard

Parents and guardians will get a private dashboard showing anonymized well-being insights — without reading private conversations. Think: "Jordan seems less anxious this week" rather than "Jordan said X." Privacy-preserving oversight.

Privacy-Preserving Anonymized Insights Guardian Controls
Mid-Term — 2027

Peer Connection Features

Rei will facilitate safe peer connections — moderated group conversations around shared interests, anonymous support circles, and moderated forums moderated by AI + human oversight. Connection without the toxicity.

Moderated Groups Anonymous Circles Shared Interests
Long-Term — 2028+

Adaptive Emotional Model

Rei's emotional intelligence will grow over time, learning from anonymized, consented interaction patterns to better detect subtle emotional shifts — anxiety spirals, depressive patterns, and joy — with increasingly nuanced, personalized responses.

Personalized AI Emotional Patterns Consent-Based Learning
Long-Term — 2028+

Research Partnership & Open Ethics

We plan to publish Rei-inspired research on teen AI companion safety, host open ethics workshops, and collaborate with academic institutions. The goal: establish industry-wide standards for teen AI safety — making Rei's approach the baseline, not the exception.

Academic Research Open Ethics Industry Standards
The Vision

What We're Building Toward

🌍

A World Where No Teen Is Alone at 2am

Rei is the first line of support when human support isn't available. She doesn't replace connection — she bridges the gap until real connections can be found.

🛡️

AI Safety as the Industry Standard

By publishing our ethics framework and safety protocols, we aim to raise the bar for every AI built for young people — so Rei becomes the template, not the exception.

💙

Technology That Heals

Rei proves that the most powerful technology is technology that makes people feel seen, safe, and supported. That's the future we're committed to building.
