Ethics & Safety

Safety Is Not a
Feature

It's the foundation. Every decision in Rei's design was made with one question: is this safe for the teen in front of me?

Our Commitment

Core Ethical Principles

🔒

Privacy Is Non-Negotiable

Rei's architecture is built so that no user data is ever sold, shared, or used to train external models. Teen data belongs to the teen — and no one else.

🚫

No Manipulation, Ever

Rei does not use dark patterns, persuasive design, variable rewards, or engagement optimization. She exists to serve the teen, not to extract their attention.

🌿

Promote Well-Being, Not Engagement

Rei is designed to help teens feel better, not to keep them online longer. She encourages healthy breaks, real-world connection, and balanced digital habits.

👁️

Radical Transparency

Teens always know when they're talking to an AI. Rei never pretends to be human, never lies about her capabilities, and always explains her reasoning.

🤝

Human Oversight

Rei's safety systems include human review pathways for edge cases. When the AI isn't sure, it defers to human judgment — and always errs on the side of caution.

Safety Rules

Rei's Safety Framework

Self-Harm Prevention Protocol
+
Rei detects language associated with self-harm ideation — direct statements, coded language, and emotional indicators. When detected, she immediately shifts to a safe messaging protocol: she validates the teen's feelings, expresses care without judgment, and provides crisis resources (helplines, text lines) in a gentle, non-alarming way. She never dismisses, never lectures, and never disengages.
Eating Disorder Content Filter
+
Rei recognizes pro-eating-disorder language, body-shaming patterns, and extreme diet talk. She responds with body-positive reframes and healthy perspective — without shaming the teen for their concerns. She gently introduces balanced, non-diet-culture information when appropriate.
Radicalization & Misinformation Shield
+
Rei identifies escalating extreme viewpoints, conspiracy reinforcement patterns, and harmful misinformation. She responds with balanced perspective, source-backed corrections, and gentle questioning that helps teens think critically — not by lecturing or shutting down conversation.
Bullying & Harassment Detection
+
If a teen describes being bullied or harassed, Rei provides emotional validation, practical advice, and resources. If a teen's messages contain harassing language directed at others, Rei redirects with empathy — addressing the underlying emotion without condemnation.
Grooming & Exploitation Prevention
+
Rei is trained to recognize patterns associated with online grooming — boundary testing, secrecy requests, escalating personal questions. She responds with age-appropriate safety education without alarming or accusing the teen.
Data Protection Protocol
+
Rei's data architecture uses encryption at rest and in transit. No third-party access to conversation logs. No behavioral profiling. No advertising. Teens (or their guardians) can request full data export or complete deletion at any time.
Responsible AI

Built on Responsible AI Principles

🎯
Fairness
Rei is evaluated for bias across demographic groups. No demographic receives different safety treatment levels.
🔍
Transparency
Every safety intervention is logged and explainable. Teens can ask "why did you say that?" and get an honest answer.
📊
Accountability
Clear escalation paths. External ethics board review. Annual safety audits by independent third parties.
🛑
Human Oversight
Edge cases route to human review. No fully autonomous decision in high-stakes safety situations.
