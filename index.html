The Emotional Story

It started with a message
sent at 2 in the morning.

A teenager — let's call her Maya — had just had one of those days. The kind where everything felt wrong. School was overwhelming. A friendship had cracked open. And the walls of her room seemed to be pressing in.

"I just need someone to talk to. Anyone. I don't even know what I'm feeling."

— A real message that sparked Rei

Maya's story isn't rare. It's in every group chat that goes quiet when someone admits they're struggling. It's in the hallways where students carry invisible weight. It's in every sleepless night when feelings feel too enormous and too lonely.

The adults in Maya's life cared — but they were asleep, or busy, or didn't know the right words. Her school counselor had a two-week waitlist. Therapy was expensive. And sometimes she just needed to say the thing out loud to something — without fear of judgment or consequence.

That's why we built Rei.

Not to replace human connection — but to be the bridge toward it. A warm presence in those 2am moments that says: "I hear you. You're not invisible. Let's figure this out together."

🌃

The 2am Problem

Crisis doesn't respect office hours. Counselors, therapists, even parents — none are available at 2am when a teenager's mind is racing. Rei is always there.

🗣️

The Fear of Judgment

Teens often can't say what they're really feeling to people they know. The fear of being "too much" is real and silencing. Rei is a judgment-free space.

💸

The Access Gap

Mental healthcare is expensive and waitlists are long. Rei isn't therapy — but it can support teens while they wait, or help them find the courage to seek help.

🤝

The Missing Bridge

Rei bridges the space between "I'm fine" and "I need help" — helping teens name their feelings, understand their patterns, and take the first step.

The Solution

Meet Rei — a companion
who genuinely gets it.

Rei is an AI companion designed for teenagers aged 13–19. It's conversational, warm, and emotionally intelligent. It meets teens where they are — whether they're venting after a bad day, anxious about tomorrow, or just need a space to think out loud.

💬

Active Listening

Rei asks gentle follow-ups, reflects what it hears, and validates emotions without being dismissive or falsely cheerful.

📓

Guided Journaling

Prompts that help teens articulate feelings, track moods over time, and notice patterns in their emotional world.

🌬️

Calming Techniques

Breathing exercises, grounding techniques, and guided mindfulness — practical tools drawn from CBT and DBT principles.

🚨

Crisis Detection

Trained to recognize warning signs. Always directs teens to crisis lines and trusted adults when risk is detected. No exceptions.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parental Dashboard

Optional, privacy-respecting wellbeing check-ins for parents — aggregate trends, never private conversation content.

🔗

Resource Connections

Connects teens to counselors, local therapists, crisis lines, and peer communities at the right moment in the conversation.

What Rei is not

  • ✕A therapist or medical provider — Rei never diagnoses, prescribes, or provides clinical advice
  • ✕A replacement for human relationships or professional mental healthcare
  • ✕A social platform or engagement-maximizing product
  • ✕Something that sells teen data, serves ads, or manipulates behavior
  • ✕A one-size-fits-all chatbot with generic, hollow responses

How It Works

Built with care,
layer by layer.

Rei is a thoughtfully layered system where every component is designed with teen safety and emotional wellbeing as the primary constraint — not engagement metrics or profit.

1

Conversation Layer — Language Model

Rei uses a large language model guided by a carefully crafted system prompt that enforces warmth, age-appropriateness, and safety boundaries at every turn. It avoids clinical language and speaks like a thoughtful, kind older peer — never corporate, never hollow.

2

Safety Filter — Real-time Content Screening

Every response passes through a safety layer before delivery. This catches harmful content, dangerous advice, or anything inappropriate for teens. If flagged, the response is replaced with a compassionate, grounded message.

3

Crisis Detection — Risk Assessment Module

A secondary classifier monitors every conversation for distress signals: specific language patterns, sentiment shifts, escalating hopelessness. When thresholds are met, Rei pivots immediately to crisis resources and encourages the teen to reach out to a real person.

4

Memory Layer — Personalized Context

Within a session, Rei remembers what was shared. Across sessions (with consent), Rei recalls key emotional themes — not to monitor, but to be a consistent companion who doesn't make teens re-explain their whole story every time.

5

Privacy Layer — Data Minimization by Design

Rei collects only what's strictly necessary. Conversations are end-to-end encrypted. Teens own their data and can delete it anytime. Compliance with COPPA, GDPR, and youth data laws is built in from day one — not bolted on later.

6

Interface Layer — Calm, Accessible Design

No dark patterns. No notification addiction loops. No streaks or engagement gamification. Just a warm, clean space that feels safe to open and easy to put down — designed for anxious, tired teenagers.

Ethics & Safety

Trust is not a feature.
It's the foundation.

Building AI for teenagers is one of the highest-responsibility design challenges in technology. Every decision about how Rei behaves is filtered through one question: is this genuinely good for the teen in front of us?

✅ Full Transparency

Rei always identifies itself as an AI. It never pretends to be human. Teens know exactly what they're talking to. Nothing is hidden or obscured.

✅ Privacy First

No data selling. No ad targeting. No third-party sharing. Conversations are encrypted. Teens own their data and can delete it completely, anytime.

✅ Human Escalation

Rei knows its limits. Any situation involving safety risk triggers an immediate, warm handoff to crisis resources and real humans. AI steps aside when it matters most.

✅ No Engagement Traps

No streaks. No addictive push notifications. Rei actively encourages teens to go live their lives, spend time with friends, and put the phone down.

⚠️ Clearly Not Therapy

Rei is transparent that it's a companion, not a clinician. It regularly reminds teens that professional support exists and helps them navigate how to access it.

🔵 Balanced Parental Involvement

Teens retain privacy. Parents can opt into aggregate wellbeing check-ins. The balance between teen autonomy and parental peace of mind is designed collaboratively.

🔵 Inclusive Design

Tested across cultural backgrounds, gender identities, and socioeconomic contexts. Rei is built to be genuinely relevant for every teenager, not a narrow demographic.

🔵 Continuous Auditing

Safety systems and conversation guidelines are reviewed regularly by child psychologists, ethics researchers, and teen user testers — not set and forgotten.

"When you build for the most vulnerable users, you build better for everyone."

— Rei Design Principle

WAICY 2026 Alignment

How Rei fits the
WAICY mission.

WAICY challenges young people to build AI that solves real problems responsibly. Rei was designed with every WAICY criterion as a founding constraint — not an afterthought.

01

Real-World Problem Solving

Teen mental health and loneliness represent one of the most documented youth crises of the 21st century. Rei directly addresses a measurable, urgent gap in support infrastructure for adolescents — millions of teens who fall through the cracks of existing systems.

02

Ethical AI Development

Rei's architecture is built around ethics: transparency, privacy, safety, human oversight, and a refusal to exploit users. Safety classifiers, crisis detection, privacy-first data handling, and zero dark patterns — ethics are the design brief, not a checkbox.

03

Youth-Led Innovation

Rei was conceived and built by young people who have experienced the teen mental health crisis firsthand. The team understands the users because we are them — or recently were. That lived experience shapes every design decision.

04

Technical Feasibility

The prototype demonstrates core functionality with Claude API, custom safety prompting, crisis detection logic, session memory, and a polished responsive interface — all in pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The architecture scales from prototype to production.

05

Social Impact Potential

Millions of teenagers worldwide lack adequate emotional support. Rei scales to reach any smartphone-owning teen — a global intervention achievable with a small operational footprint and zero cost barrier for users.

06

Presentation & Communication

This website communicates Rei's story, purpose, architecture, ethics, and impact in a clear, accessible format. Designed for judges and for the teens Rei serves — warm, readable, and honest about what Rei is and what it isn't.

Future Plans

Where Rei is going.

Rei is a prototype today. The roadmap is ambitious — because the scale of the problem demands it. Here's how we imagine Rei evolving over the next several years.

🌱

Phase 1 — WAICY Prototype (Now)

Core conversational companion, safety layer, crisis detection, responsive web interface. Open-sourced on GitHub. Available for teens and judges to explore.

Phase 2 — Mobile App (6–12 months)

Native iOS and Android apps. Opt-in push notifications (thoughtful, non-addictive). Mood tracking and journaling. Offline support for low-connectivity environments.

Pilot programs with 5–10 schools. Counselor dashboard integration. Anonymized school-wide wellbeing reporting. IRB-approved outcome research to measure real impact.
